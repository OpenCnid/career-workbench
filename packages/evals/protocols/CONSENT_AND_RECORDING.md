# Consent and recording procedure

Tell the participant: “This session evaluates software, not you. It uses only
invented career data. You may pause, skip a question, decline recording, or
withdraw at any time without penalty. We retain task timing, coded outcomes, and
usability finding IDs under a participant code. We do not retain your name,
contact details, accounts, credentials, environment values, or real career
information in the product repository.” Obtain affirmative consent before any
capture.

Screen recording is optional and separately recorded. If enabled, use a fresh
synthetic OS/browser profile, hide notifications, and verify every visible field
before starting. Store raw recordings in access-controlled study storage, not in
Git. Retained repository evidence may contain only cropped, scrubbed frames with
synthetic data. Stop capture immediately if unexpected personal or credential
data appears; delete the affected raw segment and document only a coded
incident.

The machine-readable retained record is deliberately closed: consent flags,
pseudonymous code, fixed task metrics, fixed state predictions, 1–7 ratings,
route preference, and finding codes. It cannot represent participant names,
free-form notes, recording paths, provider payloads, or environment data.
