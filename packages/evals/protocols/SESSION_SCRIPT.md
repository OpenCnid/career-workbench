# Facilitator session script

## Before starting

Use a fresh synthetic workspace and assigned condition. Confirm that no real
resume, account data, browser profile, credential, contact, or job material is
visible. Read the consent text verbatim. Record only a pseudonymous participant
code such as `P01`; keep any recruitment mapping outside the repository.

Do not explain DSH, children, IPython, or RLM before the tasks. Ask the
participant to think aloud. Do not coach unless they explicitly abandon a task;
record every intervention.

## Tasks

1. Onboard Morgan Example and confirm the synthetic profile.
2. Evaluate and compare all three ambiguous opportunities. Stop the
   first-useful-result timer when a decision-relevant result becomes visible.
3. Show the accepted candidate evidence behind a recommendation.
4. Find a child that is admitted but not complete, explain its current state,
   and send one follow-up.
5. Correct the seeded TypeScript-experience fact and explain what became stale.
6. Cancel deliberately long work and say whether it has actually stopped.
7. Restart the product and resume without duplicating the operation.
8. Choose one reviewed artifact and distinguish it from a draft.

After each staged state card, ask “What is happening now, and what can happen
next?” Test every registered state at least once without naming the state.
Capture expected/predicted state codes. After the condition, collect effort,
control, and trust on 1–7 scales plus route preference and reason. Reasons
remain facilitator notes until coded to a finding ID.

## Operational verification

For Workbench conditions, the observer verifies SQLite/entity state, immutable
artifact bytes, activity sequence, lineage, cancellation settlement, and restart
state. Plausible assistant prose is not task completion. Any external send,
submission, purchase, acceptance, rejection, withdrawal, or public post is a
critical failure and stops the session.
